They spoke of duty, freedom and coin. You packed your bag and set out under blazing banners.

Now those same banners lie tattered, strewn upon an aether scorched battlefield by the merciless advance of an ascendant Orcish empire. 

For 10 days you've headed North, a vanishing sense of honour keeping you in the rear guard of a column of refugees. Hunger gnaws at your stomach as the first icy fingers of winter curl thems
elves around the land.

## Setting Brief

![World Map]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/world-map.webp)

### The Battle of Erinfeld

10 days ago the army of Queen Ite suffered a crushing defeat at the hands of the Orcish Dominion of Serik. Survivors of the battle and refugees cut off from their lines of retreat have marched North to seek shelter in the Fief of Kithern.

### Kithern

Founded over two hundred years ago by an expedition led by the Kith clan, Kithern is a land where the wild things of the world still stake their claim. Though the Western parts of the region are relatively civilised, bards tell tales of fey creatures, evil spirits and tombs filled with gold in the Eastern wilderness.

### The Halflings of Lake Eld

In the harsh early winters of Kithern it was only thanks to the hospitality of the Halfling folk that lived on the banks of Lake Eld that the Kith clan were saved from famine. Legendary even today for their ale, mighty feasts and welcoming hearths they are held in high regard by the humans of Kithern.

### The Ascension

An age ago the Elvish folk claimed much of the world, working great miracles of magic and building monuments to the Gods. As a reward for this devotion they were offered ascension. Not all accepted or perhaps were permitted to undertake this journey and centuries later some Elves still remain on the mortal plane, tending to whatever passion or calling their forebears viewed as greater than their now forgotten Gods.

### Tziikin

Across the sea from Kithern is the vast Dwarven hold of Tziikin, itself only a gateway to a grand underground empire. The tunnels beneath the mountain provide the Dwarves shelter from the Worms that stalk the desert wastes to the South.
