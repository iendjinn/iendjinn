The piercing shriek of the vibechete cutting through synth flesh died down as Jerome kicked the defunct infiltrator android off the gantry where it tumbled for a short time in the low gravity environment of the massive room that had served as the Canyon Heavy hacker collective's HQ.

From behind came a few disciplined bursts of smart rifle fire, echoing in the cavernous space. The comm channel crackled:
"R0SA how much longer are you and our new friend going to be, running short on bullets..."
Andere's calm voice was interrupted by several dull explosions. "Seems Morgan still has plenty of grenades left though" he concluded.

"In the end, despite all our skill I'm not really sure why we beat Monarch's assault" said Angus, floating in the zero-g of the rebuilt Canyon Heavy bunker. Idly he rubbed the skin grafts that covered his cryo-burned skin. "It was almost as if the malware programmes lost their integrity and began unravelling. What was a coordinated bot net just collapsed into chaos."

R0SA's mechanical face did the closest thing it could to a frown, "Reviewing your logs this does line up exactly with when the nuke went off on the Deep, though how Monarch would have been able to transmit such vast quantities of data instantaneously over such a distance is a mystery."

"Perhaps our ex-Cloudbank contacts down on Cylon might have some answers" mused Angus, "either way I need to check in with Yandee, the first shuttles of new arrivals are due and will need to be swept for cybermod infections. Catch you later R0SA". Angus flipped neatly in the zero-g and kicked off from the workstation.
